🌱 MoistureMapper: Autonomous Soil Moisture Mapping

Overview

Soil moisture is a key variable for agricultural management, irrigation optimization, and environmental monitoring. However, existing sensing approaches struggle to provide accurate, high-resolution measurements at field scale.

MoistureMapper is an autonomous unmanned ground vehicle designed for in-situ soil moisture mapping. It uses Time Domain Reflectometry (TDR) sensing combined with a force-aware direct push drilling mechanism to collect reliable subsurface measurements.

The platform integrates a rugged mobile base, RTK-GNSS localization, autonomous waypoint navigation, and probe insertion depth estimation to operate across heterogeneous field conditions.

Adaptive Sampling

The system uses Gaussian Process–based adaptive sampling to continuously update soil moisture maps in real time. Future sampling locations are selected by balancing information gain and travel cost, enabling efficient exploration of the environment.

Results

Lab, field, and simulation experiments demonstrate robust autonomous operation in outdoor environments. The adaptive sampling strategy reduces travel distance by up to 30% compared to greedy methods while maintaining high-resolution soil moisture mapping performance.

These results highlight the potential of autonomous robotic in-situ sensing for scalable agricultural monitoring.

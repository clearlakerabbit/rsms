Robotic Soil Moisture Sampling Project(RSMS)

Soil moisture is a key variable for agricultural management, irrigation optimization, and environmental monitoring, but existing sensing approaches remain limited in their ability to provide accurate, high-resolution measurements at field scale. This paper presents MoistureMapper, an autonomous unmanned ground vehicle for in-situ soil moisture mapping using Time Domain Reflectometry (TDR) sensing integrated with a force-aware direct push drilling mechanism. The system combines a rugged mobile platform, RTK-GNSS localization, autonomous waypoint navigation, and probe insertion depth estimation to enable reliable volumetric water content measurements at subsurface-levels and across heterogeneous field conditions. In addition, the platform incorporates Gaussian Process–based adaptive sampling methods that update soil moisture maps online and select future sampling locations by balancing information gain and travel cost. Lab, field, and simulation studies are used to evaluate the robotic sensing platform and adaptive sampling framework. Results demonstrate robust autonomous operation in outdoor environments and show that the adaptive sampling strategies reduce travel distance by up to 30\% compared with greedy sampling while supporting efficient high-resolution soil moisture mapping. These results highlight the potential of autonomous robotic in-situ sensing for scalable agricultural moisture monitoring.
